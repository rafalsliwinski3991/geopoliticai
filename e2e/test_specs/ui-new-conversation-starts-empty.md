# A new conversation starts empty

## Preconditions
- Logged in as the E2E test user.

## Steps
1. Open `/chat`.
2. Press `New conversation` in the sidebar.

## Expected results
- The page heading is "New conversation".
- The conversation holds no answer and no research progress list.
- The question box is empty and the `Send` button is enabled.
- The daily research meter shows the same number of turns used as before step 2.
- No error message is shown.
