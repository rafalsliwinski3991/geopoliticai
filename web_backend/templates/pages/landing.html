What happens after you press send

The first step chooses the route. Routing and conversation are free; research spends turns from your daily allowance: one for an ordinary answer, more for a deep analysis, and all of them for a forecast.

1

Your question is routed

A geopolitical question goes to research. A follow-up like “go deeper on the cable incidents” is answered directly, without sources. A request for a written report takes its own route, and so does an explicit request for a forecast (“give me a forecast: will …?”), which a council of seven AI models votes on.

Routing is free

2

The list is searched, the pages are read

Three batches of searches run across the {{ total_domains }} domains. Up to ten of the results are fetched and the article text is extracted — not the headline, not the search snippet.

Nothing outside the list is opened

3

The answer is written from that text

One model pass over what was actually read, streamed to you as it is written. If every page candidate fails to load or extract, you get an error that names the step that failed, not a confident guess.

One turn spent

A report stops and asks before it is written

Long pieces are expensive to read and worse to skim when the structure is wrong. So the outline comes first, and nothing is written until you reply.

PoliticalAgent · mid-conversation

…and that closes the researched exchange: sources read, answer written. A report can be requested after such an exchange.

You

Write me a report on Baltic energy security since 2022.

PoliticalAgent · waiting for your reply

Here is the outline I would write. Reply to approve, revise, or cancel.

  1. Grid synchronisation with continental Europe
  2. LNG terminals and the end of piped Russian gas
  3. Undersea cable and pipeline incidents, 2023–2025
  4. What each government has committed to next
You

Approved, but merge three and four and add Finland.

Revisions

You can revise the outline twice. After that the report is written from the outline as it stands, so a conversation cannot loop.

When it is done

The finished report arrives with Download .md and Copy. It stays in the thread, so you can come back to it tomorrow.

What it will not do

The useful version of this list is the honest one. These are design decisions, not gaps waiting to be filled.

Invent a source

Every citation is a page that was fetched and read in that turn.

Answer without every page intact

A page the fetch could not read is dropped. Only a turn with nothing readable at all is an error — never a quieter answer. A forecast that some failed searches or its time limit cut short says so in its answer.

Reach outside the list

No blogs, no social posts, no press releases, no page you sent it. A forecast reads its own list instead: twelve forecasters' sites and one forecaster's public YouTube videos.

Forecast unless you ask

It forecasts only when you ask for a forecast. A council of seven AI models votes on dated claims from the forecasters' own writing, and a forecast spends 20 of your 50 daily research turns.

Work in other languages yet

Questions and answers are English. The sources are English-language.

Take long inputs

A question is capped at 2,000 characters, which is roughly one page.
