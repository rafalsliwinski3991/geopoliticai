Baltic energy security since 2022

You

What has actually changed in Baltic electricity supply since the 2025 desynchronisation?

PoliticalAgent

The three Baltic grids left the Soviet-era BRELL ring and synchronised with continental Europe, which ended their dependence on Russian frequency control. Reporting since then describes a grid that is technically independent but thinner: reserve capacity is now bought on European balancing markets rather than drawn from a neighbour, and prices move with them.
